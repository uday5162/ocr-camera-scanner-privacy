Privacy Policy

OCR Camera Scanner for Android · Last updated October 29, 2026

OCR Camera Scanner (“the app”) reads text from your camera and from images you choose. It is built to do that on your device, without sending your images or their text anywhere.

Camera and images

  • The app uses your camera only while the app is open, to find text in the viewfinder.
  • Camera frames, images you pick with the system photo picker and images you share to the app are processed on your device. They are not uploaded, stored on a server, or shared with us or anyone else.
  • The app keeps no copies of your images. A photo is saved only when you tap Save to Photos, and it is saved to your device’s photo gallery, where you control it.

Document scanning

When you scan a document, Google Play services shows the scanning screen (camera, edge detection and clean-up) on your device, and the app receives only the finished page images. The app reads their text on your device to make the PDF searchable, saves the PDF to the Download/OCR Scanner folder on your device, and then deletes the page images. Your pages are not uploaded. The My PDFs screen lists the PDFs the app saved in that folder; deleting one there deletes the file. In the version with Pro, the number of PDFs you've scanned is counted on your device to apply the free limit.

Text you copy, share or open

Text you copy goes to your device’s clipboard. Text, links, email addresses and phone numbers you share or open are passed to the app you choose (for example a browser, email app or dialer), which handles them under its own privacy policy.

Pro and scan history

Some versions of the app offer Pro, an optional one-time purchase handled entirely by Google Play; we never see your payment details. The app only remembers on your device whether Pro is unlocked. With Pro, the text of recent scans is kept in a history on your device so you can copy it again. It is not uploaded or backed up, and you can delete single scans or clear the whole history in the app. Extra languages are downloaded once through Google Play services; your images and text are still processed on your device.

Data we collect

We do not collect, store or sell personal data. The app has no accounts, no ads and no analytics of its own.

The app uses Google ML Kit for on-device text recognition. To monitor and improve the library, ML Kit sends Google diagnostic and usage information such as device model and Android version, the app’s package name and version, a per-installation identifier, performance metrics and error codes. It does not send your images or the recognized text. This data is encrypted in transit and is not shared with third parties. Details: ML Kit data disclosure.

Permissions

  • Camera: to read text live. You can deny it and still use the app with images.
  • Storage (Android 9 and older only): to save a photo to your gallery when you ask.
  • Internet: used only by ML Kit for the diagnostics described above.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.
